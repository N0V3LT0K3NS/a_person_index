# Research Contribution Guide

The research stream exists to refine mappings and protocols without turning the canonical registry into a dump of raw user material.

## Default collection posture

- prefer structured judgments over free text
- prefer normalized result atoms over raw reports
- prefer distilled observations over chat transcripts
- prefer anonymized aggregates over person-identifying records

## Contribution types

The current research contribution models are:

- `Mapping Vote`
- `Pairwise Relation Judgment`
- `Result Atom Bundle`
- `Distilled Observation`
- `Protocol Feedback`

## Promotion path

Research evidence should move through stages before it affects house synthesis:

1. anecdotal or normalized input
2. aggregated or clustered pattern
3. reviewed
4. promoted to house inference, interaction hypothesis, or protocol revision

## Preferred GNOMY return payloads

When a downstream runtime contributes back, prefer:

- normalized result atom bundles
- mapping votes
- pairwise relation judgments
- distilled observations
- protocol feedback

These are easier to review, aggregate, and promote than raw personal corpora.

## What not to do

- Do not place raw user chats in canonical framework records.
- Do not treat one user's feedback as ontology truth.
- Do not convert downstream protocol output directly into canonical claims.
- Do not erase provenance when a research observation informs a house mapping.

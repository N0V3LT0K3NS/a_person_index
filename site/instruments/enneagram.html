Constructs

  • Type One
    A reform-oriented strategy organized around restraint, improvement, and correctness.
  • Type Two
    A relationship-oriented strategy organized around helpfulness, approval, and connection.
  • Type Three
    An achievement-oriented strategy organized around performance, image, and adaptation.
  • Type Four
    An identity-oriented strategy organized around depth, uniqueness, and longing.
  • Type Five
    A withdrawal-oriented strategy organized around competence, privacy, and cognitive mastery.
  • Type Six
    A security-oriented strategy organized around vigilance, loyalty, and threat management.
  • Type Seven
    An expansive strategy organized around possibility, stimulation, and avoidance of constriction.
  • Type Eight
    A force-oriented strategy organized around autonomy, intensity, and resistance to control.
  • Type Nine
    A harmony-oriented strategy organized around stability, merger, and conflict minimization.

Source claims

  • The system describes nine core personality strategies or types.
  • Type descriptions are commonly framed through core motivations, fears, desires, and defensive patterns.
  • The framework often claims that each type has characteristic growth and stress dynamics.

Notes

# Enneagram of Personality

## What it is
A typological framework organized around nine core strategies often described through motives, fears, desires, and defenses.

## Why it matters
In this registry, Enneagram is valuable because it captures identity and motive language that many more psychometric frameworks describe only indirectly. It is also a strong example of a system that is culturally sticky despite a weaker evidence base.

## What it is good for
- motive exploration
- coaching language
- developmental reflection
- relational patterning
- identity narrative analysis

## What it is weaker at
- psychometric grounding
- standardized measurement
- low-interpretation administration
- clean construct commensurability

## Common misuse
Using type as destiny language or as an overconfident total explanation of the person.

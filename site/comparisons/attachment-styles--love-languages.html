Recorded Crosswalks

  • Attachment Style Frameworks -> The Five Love Languages [complementary_non_equivalent / medium / high]
    Attachment styles and Love Languages both operate in relationship discourse, but attachment targets security and regulation patterns while Love Languages targets preferred expressions of care.
    Useful comparative link for relational frameworks that are often conflated in popular discourse.
  • Attachment Anxiety -> Words of Affirmation [loose_overlap / medium / medium]
    Higher attachment anxiety often heightens sensitivity to reassurance and verbal confirmation, which can overlap with a felt importance of affirming language, though the constructs are not equivalent.
    This is a contextual overlap, not a translation between insecurity and preference.
  • Attachment Avoidance -> Physical Touch [inverse_tendency / medium / medium]
    Attachment avoidance can coincide with reduced comfort around closeness and touch, but a lower touch preference is neither necessary nor sufficient for avoidant attachment.
    Useful only as a cautionary comparative pattern, not as a diagnostic shortcut.
  • The Five Love Languages -> Attachment Style Frameworks [loose_overlap / medium / high]
    Love Languages and attachment frameworks both show up in relationship discourse, but they address different questions: preferred expressions of care versus security and regulation dynamics.
    Useful relational crosswalk for agents navigating popular relationship frameworks.

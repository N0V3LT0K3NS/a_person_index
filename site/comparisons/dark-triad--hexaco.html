Recorded Crosswalks

  • Dark Triad -> HEXACO Personality Inventory [inverse_tendency / medium / high]
    Dark Triad constructs frequently sit in inverse relation to prosociality-related HEXACO patterns, especially around Honesty-Humility, but they are not reducible to a single HEXACO domain.
    House comparison highlighting an important aversive-traits versus honesty-humility contrast.
  • Narcissism -> Honesty-Humility [inverse_tendency / medium / medium]
    Grandiose entitlement and self-importance often pull against the modesty and fairness facets central to HEXACO Honesty-Humility, though narcissism also carries variance not captured by that single domain.
    A directional contrast, not a full reduction.
  • Machiavellianism -> Honesty-Humility [inverse_tendency / high / high]
    Machiavellianism's manipulativeness and instrumental cynicism cut strongly against the sincerity and fairness profile associated with HEXACO Honesty-Humility.
    One of the clearest construct-level cross-system contrasts in the registry.
  • Psychopathy -> Agreeableness [inverse_tendency / high / high]
    Psychopathy's callousness and antagonism often sit opposite the forgiveness and gentleness emphasized by HEXACO Agreeableness, while still extending beyond that domain in impulsivity and fearlessness.
    Useful comparative anchor for aversive versus prosocial trait structure.
  • Honesty-Humility -> Dark Triad [inverse_tendency / high / high]
    Honesty-Humility often moves inversely to manipulative, exploitative, and entitled tendencies that cluster in Dark Triad measures.
    Construct-to-instrument mapping because the inverse relation spans multiple dark-trait constructs.
